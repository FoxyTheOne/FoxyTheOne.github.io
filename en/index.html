About me

I'm from Minsk, Belarus. I once studied Android development and later took a software testing course. Today programming is my hobby, and I enjoy it a lot.

I make the kind of apps I'd like to use myself: clear, pretty, and respectful of your time and your data. Every app is also a chance for me to learn something new.

I'm not a studio, just one person with a laptop. So things don't happen fast, but they're made with care and attention to detail.
